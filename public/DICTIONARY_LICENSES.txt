This application bundles word data derived from:

- JMdict, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG)
  License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html

- SudachiDict, Works Applications Co., Ltd.
  License: Apache License 2.0
  https://github.com/WorksApplications/SudachiDict

The bundled dictionary is a derived, normalized word-to-reading index.
